urban-mobility-simulation
=========================

A simulation platform for shared mobility services, such as ride hailing and
vehicle sharing, and for the infrastructure services they use, such as charging.

Installation, the ``umosim`` CLI and a quickstart are in the
`README <https://github.com/IS3UniCologne/urban-mobility-simulation#readme>`_.
